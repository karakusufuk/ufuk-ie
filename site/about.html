I design it. Then I build it.

For six years I ran my own design studio, Lizbon Creative, with a team of eight. I know what running a business takes, and I know a website has one job: to bring in work.

Over fifteen years, my team and I designed and built for some of Turkey’s best-known companies. For the Palmet Energy Group (Palmet, Palgaz and Palen) we delivered every site, at home and abroad, along with the full B2B and B2C software behind them: dealer networks, subscription systems, all of it. We built for machine manufacturers like Revomac, building-materials leaders like ODE and Parex, and dozens of brands across textiles, plastics, metal, packaging and lifts.

Then I moved to Ireland. Today I work end to end: I design every screen, then build and ship it myself. There is no handover between designer and developer, so nothing gets lost in between. AI tools let me move at the speed of a team; every decision is still mine.

KUS

The clearest proof is KUS, a warehouse system I designed and built for a large distribution centre in Ireland, solving a real problem on a real floor.

I work with businesses in Ireland and across Europe, in English or Turkish. You know your business. Let’s talk about what it needs online.
