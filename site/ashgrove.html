AshgroveHouse

A Georgian country house selling themed weekends: spa by day, live music by night.

Sector
Country house hotel, Kilkenny
Role
Design and build
Built with
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Claude Code
Visit the live site ↓

The weekend

One weekend, hour by hour

The hotel sells weekends, not rooms. I laid out a whole weekend as a timeline with a photograph for each hour, so a guest can picture Friday night before booking it.

Two themes, one price

Couples comparing hotels get lost in add-ons and room rates. I showed every weekend as one package with both nights, what is included and how many rooms are left, so there is nothing left to compare.

The house

Book from the house plan

Most small hotels sell a room type from a list. I let guests choose the actual room on the plan of the house, seeing where it sits and how quiet or lively it is, the way you pick a seat at a concert. At Ashgrove the house is the reason to come, so the plan sells it.

The Orangery spa

Spa treatments are usually booked at the front desk on arrival, if at all. I put them beside the rooms with times and prices, so the spa is booked together with the stay.
