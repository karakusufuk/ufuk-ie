Finding the car

The filters Irish buyers use

Irish buyers ask the same questions on every forecourt: one owner, service history, kilometres, gearbox. I built the filters from those questions, and the button counts matches as you choose, so no search ever ends in “no results”.

Every angle from the list

Buyers open listing after listing just to see the other side of a car. I let them turn each car through its angles by moving across the photo in the list, so they compare more cars in less time and open only the ones they mean.

Proof

A passport for every car

Used car buyers fear what they can’t see. I gave every car a passport, from first registration through owners, NCT and services to the workshop’s inspection, so the dealer sells on proof instead of promises.

What our workshop found

Every dealer says their cars are checked. I showed this dealer’s workshop findings part by part, what was checked and what was replaced, so the workshop, normally a cost, becomes the reason to buy here.

The full record

Buyers usually have to request a car’s history and wait for it. I put the whole record on one dated line on the car’s page, so there is nothing to ask for.

The monthly price, honestly

Most buyers think in monthly payments but don’t trust them. I made deposit and term change the figure instantly, with the full cost written underneath, so the buyer sees the total and trusts the monthly.
